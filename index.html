Partnership framework and full asset schedule · 2027 · United Kingdom.

Partnership Framework

Term1 year
One-year partnership (2027).
  • January 1, 2027 – December 31, 2027
Franchises & Events
  • Three (3) Events hosted in the UK:
  • One (1) MMA Event
  • Two (2) Boxing Events
Territories
United Kingdom
Category Exclusivity
Cloudbet is the exclusive regional betting partner across all MVP Events broadcast into the United Kingdom for the full Term. MVP will not sell the betting category on any MVP Event to a third party in this market.
Financial Commitment
  • £500,000 — inclusive of three (3) Events in territory

Partnership Assets

01

Trademark Rights & Official Designations

  • Use of MVP official marks across all marketing platforms
  • ‘Exclusive Betting Partner of MVP in the United Kingdom’
  • Non-exclusive designations: ‘Official Partner of MVP’
02

Broadcast Integrations

  • Custom broadcast opportunities per event: promotional billboards, lower thirds, Fight Presenter and Tale of the Tape graphics, live data integration
  • Applies at all three (3) Events
  • All of the above subject to approvals with the broadcaster
  • All broadcast display limited to feeds distributed solely within the Territory
03

In-Arena Branding

  • 2 × large canvas
  • 2 × vertical bumper
  • 2 × horizontal bumper
  • 2 × external bumper
  • LED screen branding throughout arena
  • 1 × activation space in the concourse
04

Presenting Partner 1x UK EVENT

  • Cloudbet to be awarded Presenting Partner status of 1 Event hosted in the Territory
  • Event marketing & promotion: Cloudbet featured and tagged (where appropriate) as presenting partner across all pre-event marketing and promotion — e.g. ‘MVP London presented by Cloudbet’
  • In-Arena additions: 1x Inner Middle Canvas position, 2x brand audio reads from announcer throughout the night
  • Additional broadcast integrations:
    • Logo and audio recognition as presenting partner on the show title card open
    • Two (2) ‘Welcome Back Bumpers’ returning from commercial break, with graphical overlay
    • Cloudbet presentation of all fight cards
    • Cloudbet mark on the Fight Clock for the first thirty (30) seconds of Main and Co-Main Events
05

Social Content Distribution

  • A total of three (3) social media posts per Event
  • Logo inclusion and tag of Cloudbet on each post
06

MVP Digital & Video Content

  • Pre-event: press conferences, weigh-ins, face-offs, promos, fighter media-day cutdowns
  • During event: rapid-turn highlight cutdowns
  • Post-event: official photos and recap videos
  • Usable across Cloudbet-owned channels, subject to agreed rules
  • Content access applies to all MVP / MMA Events across the Term, in and out of Territory
07

Watch & Bet Streaming

  • Non-exclusive live event Watch & Bet rights for the Term — live event stream for distribution on Cloudbet platforms, via MVP
08

Athlete Ambassador Program

  • Two (2) MVP fighters for the term to serve as Cloudbet ambassadors, included
  • Roles agreed with MVP — content, fight-kit logo and appearances
09

VIP Hospitality & Access Cloudbet to receive per event

  • Five (5) VIP tickets
  • Five (5) GA tickets
  • Optional extras: on-stage meet and greet at weigh-ins; backstage tour on fight night; access to press conferences and face-offs
10

Account Management

  • A dedicated MVP Account Director for the Term
